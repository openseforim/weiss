About this text

Vol. 1, pp. 227 (Daat scan of a printing after 1891). One row is one heading or one paragraph of the print; Weiss's own footnotes follow the text, numbered through the part (the print numbers them page by page). Boxes marked "p. N" in the Hebrew show where a printed page begins; they link to the page in the Daat scan, and the Scan button at the top shows it beside the text. References in [gray brackets] are the translator's. ⚠ marks an uncertain rendering. The Hebrew was checked against the scan line by line; spelling and abbreviations are as printed.
