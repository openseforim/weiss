v1_11_04:2) In Berakhot 1:2 it is said that the time of the morning Shema is from when one can distinguish between blue and white. This sign, it seems, was given by the Tannaim after the destruction; and perhaps they had some sign that depended on the existence of the Temple, like what was taught (Yoma 32b)⚠16: "When the sun rises, sparks come out of it" (from the candelabrum), "and all know that it is the time of the reading of the Shema"; and after the destruction they were forced to give another sign. For the evening Shema they left the old sign, since even after the destruction there were those who ate terumah in purity. But even so this sign too was exchanged by the Tannaim after the destruction for another sign, as is explained in the Talmud.
